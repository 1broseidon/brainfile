## Description
Copy says what it does, in plain words: never casual, cute or vague
Every label, button, tip, line and message in Brainfile (desktop, phone, CLI) names the action or the fact in the person's words: what pressing it will do, or what is true now. No idioms, jokes, shrugs or reassurance. 'Done, let it go' (the Merge button once its branch is already merged) is the example not to follow; it becomes 'Merged · remove worktree'. Why: Brainfile is an instrument used all day by someone easily pulled off course (design.md, Calm). A breezy phrase makes them stop to work out what it means, and a vague one hides what the control does. Plain words are also what an agent reads back from the window.
