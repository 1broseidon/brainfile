## Description
The calm list is a mode of the list, chosen once for the window
The ADHD-friendly reading of the task list (task-43, after brainfile-mobile's board) is a second reading of the existing list, not a fifth view in the band: one row per task with its status box, title and at most one signal (restingSignal), groups nearest done first, the backlog and done resting folded, a group at a glance with `N more`. It belongs to the reader, not to a board, so it is one switch for the whole window (Settings › List › Calm list, ⇧V, the palette), remembered, and off until turned on so the full rows stay what they were. desktop/design.md, Calm list, is its spec; the outline rules and the j / k walk are shared with the full list through app.listGroups.
