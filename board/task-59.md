## Description
When the phone is linked, the board's chat history is the same on both. The desktop keeps the last 30 chats on this machine; the phone shows that list and can open, continue or start a thread, and the other side sees it. Discuss threads stay task-scoped and titled `Discuss <task-id>` (decision-6). Hiding the desktop chat still keeps the thread (decision-8). Unlinked, the phone has no chat history of its own. Labels stay plain (decision-10).
