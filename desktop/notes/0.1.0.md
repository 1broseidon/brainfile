The first release of Brainfile Desktop: your repositories' boards in one window, and the agents you already use working them.

- **Boards from your repositories.** Every project's board on its branch, read and written as the CLI does, with its decisions, its sister boards and its health in the strip.
- **A calm list.** One signal a row, nearest done first; the full list is ⇧V away.
- **Hand to….** Any ACP agent on your Mac (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, Antigravity, or one from the ACP registry) works a task in its own worktree, forked from the trunk, and waits on what it depends on.
- **Ready to merge.** What can merge is at the top of the list and of the feed. Merge runs the task's checks on the result and lands it on the trunk without touching your folder; a conflict goes back to the agent that did the work.
- **The board's chat.** Ask about the board, add and change tasks, plan an epic with its order and checks, and hand work off; code is left to hand-offs.
- **Discuss.** Talk a task over with an agent before anyone builds it.
- **Updates.** Brainfile checks for new releases and updates itself in place.

For Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
